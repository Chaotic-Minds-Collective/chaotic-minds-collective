Origin

Meet Craniac

Craniac is made of every abandoned idea ever thought. The ideas that got shelved, dismissed, or forgotten didn't die - they drifted until they found each other, stitched themselves into a small stubborn skull, and lit up. His brain glows with all of them. The heart in his ribcage is every creator's love for the ideas nobody else understood. The wisp tail is made of the ideas that refused to go out.

If your head is full of chaos... you've got a Craniac.
